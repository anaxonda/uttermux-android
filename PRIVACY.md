# UtterMux privacy policy

UtterMux does not contain advertising, analytics, or tracking. Local text and
local voice synthesis remain on the device. Pocket reference recordings and
created voice profiles remain in private application storage and are never sent
to an online provider.

When the user explicitly selects an online voice, the requested text and the
voice/language parameters are sent to that provider. The provider may charge
for synthesis and applies its own privacy policy. Paid previews require a
one-time confirmation. Cloud voices are never added to automatic fallback
routes without the user's action.

API credentials are encrypted with Android Keystore, excluded from Android
backup and device transfer, and can be removed from the provider's Settings
card. The optional KOReader bridge listens only on IPv4 loopback at
127.0.0.1:5000.
